# Review Shop Product Context

Review Shop is a Vietnamese marketplace connecting clients with freelancers through
structured work, review content, milestones and transparent payouts.

## Audience

- Clients who need reliable execution and visible progress.
- Freelancers who want qualified opportunities and reputation built from completed work.
- Admins who need clear, accountable operational workflows.

## Product promise

Make collaboration easier to trust by making expectations, evidence, progress and
money explicit.

## Brand commitments

- Clarity before decoration.
- Evidence before hype.
- Human, direct Vietnamese copy.
- Calm confidence rather than startup theatrics.
- Vietnamese text must render with stable, explicitly loaded typography across
  supported browsers and operating systems.

## Typography requirement

- The product font is **Be Vietnam Pro** for Vietnamese body text, headings,
  buttons, inputs, navigation and Ant Design components.
- It must be loaded explicitly in `index.html` with Vietnamese glyph support.
- The fallback stack is `Segoe UI`, `Noto Sans`, then `Arial`, sans-serif.
- Do not use Georgia, Times New Roman or another serif font for Vietnamese UI
  headings because diacritic rendering must remain consistent across platforms.

## Surface modes

- Homepage: persuade through proof and a clear next step.
- Auth and onboarding: reassure and reduce uncertainty.
- Dashboards: operate quickly with dense, scannable information.
